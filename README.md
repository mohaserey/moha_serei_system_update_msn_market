Every participation counts.🌟 Overview / សេចក្តីផ្តើមThe Mohaserei Marketplace & Merchant Network (moha_serei_system_update_msn_market.html) represents Layer 2 (Economic Transaction Network) of the Mohaserei Network (MSN) Enterprise Architecture (MSN-DIGI-001). It serves as the national commercial exchange platform connecting agricultural cooperatives, handicrafts, logistics providers, retail shop owners, and industrial enterprises across all 25 capitals and provinces of Cambodia.Through this module, merchants and consumers participate directly in the participation-based economy, turning everyday commercial transactions into verified Universal Economic Scores (UES) and distributed asset growth.🏛️ Core Architecture & Frameworks / ក្របខ័ណ្ឌស្ថាបត្យកម្មគ្រឹះLayer 2 Economic Transaction Network (MSN-DIGI-001): Manages national marketplaces, retail networks, and supplier distribution channels within a single auditable ledger.Business Architecture Flow (MSN-EAB-001 & MSN-BPM-001):
$$\text{Supplier} \longrightarrow \text{Distributor} \longrightarrow \text{Merchant} \longrightarrow \text{Consumer} \longrightarrow \text{Reward Engine} \longrightarrow \text{Economic Value}$$Participation Category 5 Integration (Business Capital): Directly empowers shop owners, handicrafts, communities, SMEs, and manufacturing factories to list products and earn Business Capital (BC - 25%) weights.🛒 Key Module Features / មុខងារសំខាន់ៗNational Commercial Metrics: Real-time tracking of active partner shops (45,200+ Merchants), agricultural/handicraft suppliers (1,850+ Units), daily transaction volume ($850,000+/day), and cumulative market UES accumulation.Featured Ecosystem Products: Showcases primary goods and services produced by network members, including community agricultural rice, handicraft souvenirs, logistics delivery services, and digital POS technology packages.Merchant & Enterprise Registry (MDR-002): Centralized directory linking shop owners and agricultural cooperatives to their respective provincial geographic locations and accumulated UES scores.Seamless Ecosystem Navigation: Direct sidebar integration linking the National Dashboard, Member Registration, Member Directory, Economy Engine, and Marketplace.🛠️ Technology Stack / បច្ចេកវិទ្យាប្រើប្រាស់Frontend: HTML5, CSS3 (Custom Grid Layouts & Responsive Flexbox), Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)Typography & Icons: Google Fonts (Kantumruy Pro) & FontAwesome 6.4.0Branding: Official Mohaserei Network logo asset (mohaserey.jpg)📂 Repository Structure / រចនាសម្ព័ន្ធឯកសារ├── moha_serei_system_update_dashboard.html     # National Impact & Analytics Dashboard
